Brand kit. Black, white, one typeface, her face.

Logos

MascotHer portrait as a flat icon. Copper hair, round glasses, freckles. Nav, hero, and the face on the Mac and iPhone app icon.

App icons are drawn in the icon composer, mascot face over her hair and shoulders. The engraved SAMANTHA / TURING medallion stays in the repo as history. No text inside any icon.

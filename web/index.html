Fair questions

Questions

Is it really private?

Yes. She runs on your Mac. Your words, files and screen never go to a server. The demo on this page is a stand-in on the web, so type nothing you would not post.

Can she break my stuff?

Not without asking. Anything that writes, sends or deletes waits for your yes. The rules.

Is she as smart as ChatGPT or Claude?

No. She is small, free and private, and says "I don't know" instead of guessing.

What does she run on?

A Mac with Apple silicon runs all of her. Windows and Linux run her picker and chat. She also answers inside Joshua Tree, an OS built from scratch.
